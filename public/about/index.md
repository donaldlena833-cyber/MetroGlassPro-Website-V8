# About MetroGlass Pro

MetroGlass Pro is a New York custom-glass company focused first on shower doors and enclosures, with related mirror, partition, repair, and select railing work. Manhattan apartments, co-ops, condos, and brownstones are a priority, and the company also serves Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx, Staten Island, and select nearby projects.

## Approach

Projects are measured in the finished space, designed around the real opening, and coordinated with the building when COIs, freight access, elevator windows, or scheduling rules apply. The public promise centers on precise fit, clean installation, direct communication, and honest guidance about whether an existing door should be repaired or replaced.
