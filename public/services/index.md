# MetroGlass Pro services

## Frameless shower doors

Custom swing, pivot, sliding, fixed-panel, corner, and neo-angle enclosures measured for the actual bathroom.

## Replacement and repair

Replacement for dated or failed enclosures, plus leak, seal, hinge, hardware, dragging-door, and poor-install remediation.

## Mirrors and partitions

Bathroom, vanity, and wall mirrors, plus residential and boutique-commercial interior glass partitions.

## Estimate preparation

Send the borough and neighborhood, building type, current photos, whether the project is new installation, replacement, or repair, approximate dimensions, preferred glass and hardware, and any COI or access rules. Pricing is project-specific; published shower-door guidance is commonly around $800 to $3,000 or more depending on configuration and finish.
