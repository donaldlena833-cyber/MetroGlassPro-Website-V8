# MetroGlass Pro projects and planning guides

MetroGlass Pro publishes real-project explanations and planning guides for Manhattan and New York City buyers. Topics include shower-door configurations, finished-tile measurement, co-op and condo coordination, office glass partitions, replacement decisions, and installation preparation.

Use the project index to compare layouts and understand what information improves an estimate. Photographs, dimensions, building type, access requirements, and desired hardware or glass make the first conversation more useful.
