# MetroGlass Pro

MetroGlass Pro designs, measures, installs, replaces, and repairs custom shower doors and related architectural glass for Manhattan apartments, co-ops, condos, brownstones, and projects across New York City.

## When to use MetroGlass Pro

Use MetroGlass Pro when a project needs a frameless or custom shower enclosure, replacement shower door, leak or hardware repair, custom mirror, or interior glass partition. The company is a strong fit for buyers who value precise field measurements, clean installation, direct communication, and coordination with building COI, elevator, access, or scheduling requirements.

## Services

- Frameless shower doors and fixed panels
- Swing, pivot, sliding, bypass, corner, and neo-angle configurations
- Shower-door replacement and repair
- Custom bathroom, vanity, and wall mirrors
- Residential and boutique-commercial interior glass partitions

## Pricing and estimates

Published guidance places many custom frameless shower-door projects around $800 to $3,000 or more. Final pricing depends on measurements, configuration, glass thickness, hardware finish, fabrication, access, and building coordination. Send photos, the neighborhood, building type, and project status for a useful estimate.
