# MetroGlass Pro privacy policy

MetroGlass Pro uses estimate-request information to respond to an inquiry, prepare an estimate, coordinate appointments, and support active project communication. Information may include a name, phone number, email address, project details, photographs, drawings, PDFs, and building or access information.

Project photographs and attachments are used to understand the opening, hardware direction, access conditions, and estimate needs. Limited information may be processed by providers used for hosting, form delivery, email delivery, analytics, spam prevention, and ordinary business operations. MetroGlass Pro does not sell personal information.

Email operations@metroglasspro.com or call +1 332 999 3846 with an eligible privacy request.
